\begin{eabstract}
%英文アブストラクト 100 words
One solution to the shortage of caregiving personnel caused by the declining birthrate and aging population is the support of independent living through activity recognition. While activity recognition typically relies on sensors such as cameras and wearables, this study investigates the real-world application of Sound Event Detection (SED) via microphones. This approach aims to complement existing sensors while ensuring privacy-conscious monitoring of the elderly. Specifically, to enhance accuracy without increasing the number of model parameters, we improved and integrated architectures for semi-supervised learning using confidence scores and domain generalization methods. The effectiveness of the proposed method was analyzed through comparative evaluation experiments against prior implementations.
